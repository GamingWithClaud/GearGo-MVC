
## 1.1 Current Problem

GearGo is an equipment rental business that currently manages its rental equipment and customer requests manually or through an inefficient tracking system. This makes it difficult for staff to keep track of which equipment is currently available, rented out, reserved, damaged, or under maintenance.

Customers may also have limited visibility of the available equipment. They may need to contact staff directly to find out whether a particular item is available and how much it costs to rent. This can result in delays and may cause confusion when several customers request the same equipment for similar dates.

The current system also places a large administrative workload on staff. Employees have to manually record rental requests, update equipment availability, keep track of customer information, and maintain rental history. Because much of this information is handled manually, there is a greater possibility of human error, duplicate bookings, missing information, and outdated availability records.

The proposed GearGo system will provide a centralized web-based platform that allows customers, staff, and managers to interact with the rental system according to their roles and permissions.

## 1.2 Affected Users

The main users affected by the current problems are:

### Customers

Customers need a simple way to browse available equipment, view prices and item information, and submit rental requests. They should not have to rely entirely on staff to determine what equipment is available.

### Staff

Staff members are responsible for the day-to-day management of rental requests and equipment. They need a system that allows them to quickly view requests, process bookings, and update equipment availability.

### Managers

Managers require greater control over the overall system. They need to manage the equipment catalogue, pricing, users, permissions, and other administrative information.

## 1.3 Business Impact

The current process can negatively affect GearGo's business operations in several ways.

Potential problems include:

- **Double bookings:** The same equipment could accidentally be rented to more than one customer for the same period.
- **Slow response times:** Staff may need to manually check records before confirming whether equipment is available.
- **Incorrect availability information:** Customers may be told that equipment is available when it has already been reserved.
- **Increased administrative workload:** Staff spend unnecessary time maintaining records and processing requests manually.
- **Limited rental history:** It can be difficult to quickly determine what equipment was previously rented, by whom, and for which dates.
- **Pricing errors:** Equipment prices may not always be updated consistently across records.
- **Poor customer experience:** Customers may become frustrated if they cannot easily view equipment and submit rental requests.
- **Difficulty monitoring equipment:** Staff and managers may have limited visibility of equipment that is damaged, unavailable, or being maintained.

## 1.4 Proposed Solution

The proposed GearGo Equipment Rental System will be a centralized web application that provides a single location for managing equipment rentals.

The system will allow customers to:

- Create and manage their accounts.
- Browse equipment categories.
- Search for available equipment.
- View equipment descriptions, images, prices, and availability.
- Select required rental dates.
- Submit rental requests.
- View the status of their rental requests.
- View their previous rental history.

Staff will be able to:

- View incoming rental requests.
- Check equipment availability.
- Approve or reject rental requests.
- Update equipment availability.
- Manage equipment that is rented, available, damaged, or under maintenance.
- View customer rental information.
- Keep rental records up to date.

Managers will have additional administrative access to:

- Add new equipment.
- Edit equipment information.
- Update rental prices.
- Remove equipment from the catalogue.
- Manage customer and staff accounts.
- Assign or change user permissions.
- Monitor the overall rental system.

## 1.5 System Boundary

The GearGo system will focus on the management of equipment rentals and the information required to process those rentals.

### Included within the system

- Customer registration and login.
- User authentication and authorization.
- Equipment catalogue management.
- Equipment categories.
- Equipment availability tracking.
- Rental requests.
- Rental request approval and rejection.
- Rental dates.
- Rental pricing.
- Customer rental history.
- Staff management functions.
- Manager administration functions.
- User account and permission management.
- Basic rental and equipment status tracking.

### Outside the initial system boundary

The following features are not part of the initial version unless they are added as future requirements:

- Physical delivery of equipment.
- Equipment repair services.
- Advanced accounting and financial reporting.
- Integration with external accounting software.
- Automated equipment tracking using GPS or IoT devices.
- Online payment gateway integration.
- Third-party marketplace integration.
