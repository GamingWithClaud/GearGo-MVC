The GearGo system will contain three main user roles. Each role will have different permissions based on the responsibilities of the user.

## 2.1 Customer

A Customer is an external user who wants to rent equipment from GearGo.

Customers will be able to browse the equipment catalogue and view information about available equipment. They can select the equipment they require, specify their preferred rental dates, and submit a rental request.

Customers will not have access to staff or manager functions such as changing equipment availability, approving rental requests, or managing other users.

### Customer permissions

Customers can:

- Register an account.
- Log in and log out.
- Browse equipment.
- Search for equipment.
- View equipment details.
- View rental prices.
- Check equipment availability.
- Submit rental requests.
- View their own rental requests.
- View rental request status.
- View their rental history.
- Update their own account information.

Customers cannot:

- Approve rental requests.
- Reject rental requests.
- Change equipment availability.
- Add or delete equipment.
- Change equipment prices.
- Manage other users.
- Change system permissions.

---

## 2.2 Staff

A Staff member is an internal GearGo employee responsible for handling the daily rental operations.

Staff members will have more access than customers because they need to process rental requests and maintain accurate equipment information.

### Staff permissions

Staff can:

- Log into the staff area.
- View incoming rental requests.
- View customer information related to requests.
- Check equipment availability.
- Approve rental requests.
- Reject rental requests.
- Update equipment availability.
- Mark equipment as rented.
- Mark equipment as returned.
- Mark equipment as damaged.
- Mark equipment as under maintenance.
- View rental history.
- Search for customers and rental records.

Staff cannot normally:

- Manage manager accounts.
- Change system-wide permissions.
- Delete manager accounts.
- Make major system configuration changes.

---

## 2.3 Manager

A Manager is a high-level administrator responsible for overseeing GearGo's equipment rental system.

Managers will have the highest level of access and will be able to manage the equipment catalogue, users, prices, and system permissions.

### Manager permissions

Managers can:

- Perform all appropriate staff functions.
- Add new equipment.
- Edit equipment information.
- Delete or deactivate equipment.
- Add and edit equipment categories.
- Change equipment rental prices.
- Manage customer accounts.
- Manage staff accounts.
- Assign user roles.
- Change system permissions.
- View rental records.
- Monitor equipment availability.
- Review system activity.

### Managers cannot:

- Access or change sensitive system information that is outside their assigned permissions.
- Make changes to the underlying application code.
- Access another manager's private login credentials.
- Bypass the system's security and authentication controls.

Manager access should be protected because changes made by managers can affect the entire rental system.
